Dowloading the hiking trails and plotting them on the evelation raster data.

I had to transform the Zion Park map to WGS84 (EPSG:4326) to ensure compatibility with OpenStreetMap (OSM) data. Initially, I was unable to query hiking trails from OSM because the map and the trail data were using different coordinate reference systems, which made spatial operations fail.

Additionally, the elevation raster data was provided in a UTM projection, so after retrieving and processing the trails in WGS84, I needed to transform everything into the UTM CRS to correctly overlay the vector and raster layers. Consistent CRS alignment was essential for accurate spatial analysis and visualization.

After a quick search on CHATGPT, I learnt that hiking trails are stored under the highway tag in OSM. Therefore, the get this information about the hiking trails in Zion National Park, so the features with highway=path needed to be queried to get all the information on walkable paths in the Zion National Park, since they are not labelled speicifcally as “hiking” trails in OSM.
